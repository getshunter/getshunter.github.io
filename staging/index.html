The problem

CI has no inner loop

Application code gets a local edit-run-debug cycle. Pipeline failures often mean push, wait, read a log, guess and push again. A late failure can cost another full build cycle, and the runner's state may be gone by the time you investigate.

Shunter brings that iteration loop to CI workflow logic, on the machine where you can inspect it.

Who Shunter is for

Windows/C++ and mixed Windows/Linux teams with long, flaky or hard-to-reproduce builds; build and release engineers; and teams planning a CI migration.

Who it's not for

macOS users, teams seeking a hosted CI service, or teams that require open-source desktop tooling. Shunter runs in your CI and its desktop app is proprietary.

Trust

Your runs stay yours

The desktop app and runner make no background telemetry or phone-home requests. A Flowbook can download files or run network-using commands you choose. Local reports are written where you choose; the Action downloads a runner and uploads configured reports and artifacts to GitHub.

Read the trust summary·Full trust page·Security policy

Pricing and licence

Runner and Action are free for everyone. Desktop is free for personal, open-source and evaluation use. Commercial desktop use is through pilots; start a conversation inGitHub Discussions. No prices are published.

Coming later: remote attach

Attaching the desktop debugger to a paused CI run through a relay is planned for a later release. It is not available in this preview.

FAQ

Preview questions

Is the first preview signed?

The first preview is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn, and the Linux AppImage needs execute permission before it will run.

Is there a macOS build?

No macOS build yet. Use a Windows or Linux machine.

Does Shunter phone home?

The desktop app and runner make no background telemetry or phone-home requests.

Can I share an HTML report?

HTML reports can contain paths, task output and environment context. Treat them as sensitive and review them before sharing.
